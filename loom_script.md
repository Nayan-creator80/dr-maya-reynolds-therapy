## 💡 Key Recording Tips for Loom Submission

1. **Camera & Audio:** Keep your camera on in the bottom corner of Loom; maintain enthusiastic eye contact and clear English pronunciation.
2. **Speed & Pacing:** Speak at a calm, natural cadence (~130 words per minute).
3. **No Jargon:** Avoid technical developer terms like "CSS flexbox", "Next.js server component", "media queries", or "DOM node". Instead use "smooth layouts", "mobile-friendly viewports", "clear visual hierarchy", and "easy navigation".
